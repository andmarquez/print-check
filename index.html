How to use this calculator

Enter values from your slicer for filament used and print time. Every cost updates live as you adjust material, energy, and machine settings.

Material settings

Enter the price and weight of your filament spool plus how many grams the print uses. Slicers like Cura, PrusaSlicer, and OrcaSlicer report estimated filament weight directly.

Energy settings

Use the print time from your slicer, your printer's power draw, and your local electricity rate. Most desktop printers pull 100–150 W; enclosed or heated-chamber machines can reach 200–400 W.

Machine wear

This optional section spreads your printer's purchase price across its usable life. A typical desktop printer lasts roughly 3,000–10,000 print hours.

Failure rate

Add a buffer for failed prints. If about 1 in 20 prints fails, set this to 5%. It applies a proportional markup across material, energy, and wear.

Tips for reducing print costs

  • Optimize infill. Dropping infill from 20% to 10% can save 15–25% of filament on many parts.
  • Pick the right material. PLA runs about $15–25/kg; specialty filaments can cost 3–5× more.
  • Reduce supports. Reorient the model to cut wasted support material.
  • Buy in bulk. Multi-packs often shave 20–40% off the per-spool price.
  • Calibrate. A dialed-in printer fails less, which directly lowers your effective cost per part.
